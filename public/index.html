GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
before you play.

How do I install Outpost?+

Tap Download for Android, then open the downloaded APK on your phone. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or files app. Outpost needs Android 10 or newer and a 64-bit ARM device. The download is the complete app, with its lessons and offline analysis included.

Does it work without an internet connection?+

Yes. Playing the computer, lessons, puzzles, openings and game analysis work offline. You only need an internet connection to download the app or import public games from Chess.com and Lichess.

Do I need an account or a subscription?+

No account or subscription is required. Outpost is free to download, and your games and learning progress stay on your device.

Can I play against other people online?+

Outpost focuses on solo play and practice. Play against the computer at different strengths, then study your games. It doesn’t include online matchmaking.

How does game review work?+

Review runs locally on your phone and highlights key moves, accuracy and opportunities to improve. Quick reviews use shorter searches; choose More detail or explore a position in Analysis for a deeper look. Review speed depends on your phone and game length.
