THE DETAILS

Made for
the love of chess.

Outpost was created by Waleed. This is an independent chess app; it is not affiliated with Chess.com or Lichess.

Your privacy

This website has no accounts, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. Hosting and download providers (Vercel and GitHub) process ordinary request information. Fonts load from Google Fonts. The app stores games, settings and learning progress on your device. Public game imports connect to Chess.com or Lichess only when you request them. Backups go to the location you choose.

Artwork and chess pieces

The illustrated chess artwork was created for Outpost with AI image generation. The Outpost logo is original project artwork.

Website chess pieces: Chessnut by Alexis Luengas, licensed under Apache 2.0. Original source and attribution. Other piece sets included in the Android app carry their own credits in Settings → Licences.

Standing on good foundations

The app includes Stockfish 19, a separate offline chess engine, under GPL v3. Puzzle data and opening names are provided by Lichess under CC0. AndroidX, Jetpack Compose and Kotlin power the Android experience.

The release includes a third-party source and licence bundle. Its Stockfish source, build instructions, network references and piece SVG sources accompany the components distributed in the APK. Full attribution is also available inside the app.

Download details

The standalone Android APK requires Android 10 or later on an ARM64 device. No separate content download is needed. Release information, file size and SHA-256 checksum are available on the GitHub release page.
